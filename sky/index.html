Sky Atlas

The sky, night by night

Everything here is for people who actually go out and look: what is up on a given evening, a guided walk through it, the 88 constellations drawn star by star, and the bright stars one page each. It all works on a phone, in the dark, with cold hands.

Plan a night

Everything below runs off the place and the night at the top of this page. Pick a day on the calendar and the panels move with it: which planets, deep-sky objects and bright stars are up that evening and until what time, and a map of the whole sky as it will look from there once night falls. Days marked ✅ are the best ones for a camp — the Moon is a thin crescent that sets early and leaves the rest of the night dark.
