Star positions, magnitudes, spectral types and parallax distances come from the Hipparcos catalogue (ESA 1997) via VizieR; the far distances are estimates, and a parallax too small to measure is reported as no distance rather than a large one. Proper names are those approved by the IAU Working Group on Star Names. Star figures are the ones the IAU publishes, drawn by Alan MacRobert for Sky & Telescope, used under CC BY 4.0; the machine-readable tracing is from d3-celestial (BSD-3-Clause). Sanskrit identifications come from the Sūrya Siddhānta, Bṛhat Saṃhitā, Siddhānta Śiromaṇi and the Vedic corpus — where a reading is contested, the page says so.
