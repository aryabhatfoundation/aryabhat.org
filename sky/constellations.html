Stars on this chart

Star figures are the ones the IAU publishes for each constellation, drawn by Alan MacRobert for Sky & Telescope, used under CC BY 4.0; the machine-readable tracing is from d3-celestial (BSD-3-Clause). Star positions, magnitudes, spectral types and parallax distances come from the Hipparcos catalogue (ESA 1997) via VizieR; proper names are those approved by the IAU Working Group on Star Names. Sanskrit identifications come from the Sūrya Siddhānta, Bṛhat Saṃhitā, Siddhānta Śiromaṇi and the Vedic corpus, each traced to the verse it rests on — where a reading is contested, the chart says so.
